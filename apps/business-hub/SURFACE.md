# Business Hub dashboard

Mode: Operate. Extends the incumbent Postiz light interface; no replacement brand.

THESIS: Operators choose a business before seeing strategy, content, credentials, or history.

OWN-WORLD: Postiz light neutral background and white work surfaces, purple primary actions, system sans typography, restrained borders, clear states.

STORY: Sign in, select or create a business, connect channels, write and activate a strategy, create/review/schedule content, give agents scoped access, inspect outcomes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact Postiz masthead, portfolio title and Add business action, then a real business table or truthful empty state. Inside a workspace, its name, timezone, publishing policy, and task tabs stay above the current work.

FORM: Portfolio table with workspace drilldown, fourth of seven grounded structures (sidebar workbench, approval inbox, calendar, portfolio table, strategy planner, task queue, activity feed). Seed 2b925341. Table staging preserves recognizable business identity at every row; no scenic or spatial challenger improved operation clarity. On phones, the optional identifier column disappears and the workspace reflows to one column.

Open decisions: launch businesses, channels, hosting destination, and publication authority. Provisional defaults are listed in PRODUCT.md. No illustrative customer data ships in production.
